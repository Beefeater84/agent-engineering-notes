A document about a decision that has been made. It is written after the decision, stored together with the code, and answers the question: what do we use and why.


Good practice for the path: `docs/adr/0007-use-supabase-rls.md`
It usually contains:
- A short title and a number. The number is important to understand the order of decisions, and that some of them may have been rewritten. "ADR-0007: Use Supabase RLS for authorization".
- Status: proposed / accepted / deprecated / superseded
- Context: what the situation is and what the constraints are
- What was decided
- Consequences: what we pay for it


ADRs set constraints for the whole project that must not be violated.
